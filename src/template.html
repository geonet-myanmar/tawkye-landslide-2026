    Calibration check

    Share of each mapped landslide that falls inside the modelled reach (a model that missed its own calibration events would be useless):

    Read this as screening, not a zoning map. It runs on a 30 m pre-event DEM, ignores volume, velocity and obstacles, and treats every slope ≥ 20° as a possible source. It answers one question: which houses are downslope of steep ground within the reach these flows actually showed? Siting decisions need a field assessment by GSM or another qualified team.
